Who it's owed to

Domestic debt is owed to lenders inside the Maldives, mostly banks and pension funds. External debt is owed abroad, to other governments, development banks and bondholders. Guaranteed debt is borrowed by state companies with the government promising to pay if they can't.

    How it got here

    Total public and publicly guaranteed debt at the end of each quarter, in billions of rufiyaa.

    How the clock works

    Official debt figures come out once a quarter. The clock starts from the latest published total and adds debt at the average pace of the most recent four quarters, so the number between releases is an estimate. When a new quarter is published, the clock resets to the official figure.
