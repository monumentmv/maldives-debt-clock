Domestic debt is owed to lenders inside Maldives, mostly banks, the central bank and the pension fund. External debt is owed abroad, to other governments, development banks and bondholders. Guaranteed debt is borrowed mostly by state-owned companies, from banks and other lenders, with the government promising to pay if they can't.

    Where a year's spending goes

    Official debt figures come out once a quarter. The clock starts from the latest published total and adds debt at the average pace of the most recent four quarters, so the number between releases is an estimate. When a new quarter is published, the clock resets to the official figure. The site checks for new data every morning.
