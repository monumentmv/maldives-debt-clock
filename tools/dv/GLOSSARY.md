# Dhivehi wording rules for the site (draft, to be checked by the owner)

Style
- Formal written Dhivehi, sentences ending in "…ެވެ" (as in news and official writing). Short sentences. Calm, plain, not sensational.
- Arabic comma "،" and question mark "؟". Full stop ".".
- Numbers stay in normal digits (152.8, 2026). Percentages written "63.3%" (the site keeps them left to right automatically).
- Money: "ރ. 152.8 ބިލިއަން" in figures (the shared code does this). In sentences "ރުފިޔާ" / "ޑޮލަރު". billion ބިލިއަން, million މިލިއަން.
- Months: ޖަނަވަރީ ފެބުރުއަރީ މާރިޗު އެޕްރީލް މެއި ޖޫން ޖުލައި އޮގަސްޓު ސެޕްޓެމްބަރު އޮކްޓޫބަރު ނޮވެމްބަރު ޑިސެމްބަރު (the shared mLabel/dayLabel already give these on Dhivehi pages).
- "at the end of June 2026" = "ޖޫން 2026 ގެ ނިޔަލަށް". "in the 12 months to July 2026" = "ޖުލައި 2026 އާ ހަމައަށް 12 މަހު".
- Arrows: "→" becomes "←" in Dhivehi. "‹ Earlier / Later ›" are mirrored.
- Proper names of loans, projects, lenders' official names, report titles and PDF names stay in English. Country names in Dhivehi.
