Where interest goes

Interest is income for whoever lent to the government. It pays for borrowing that has already happened, so in the year it is paid it does not build a classroom, buy medicine or pay a new salary.

Lenders in Maldives

Commercial banks, MMA, the pension fund and other financial institutions hold most Treasury bills and bonds. Their interest stays in the economy but goes to lenders rather than public services.

Lenders abroad

Interest on external loans, sukuk and international bonds leaves the country in foreign currency and draws on reserves.

What it crowds out

Every rufiyaa of interest is a rufiyaa the budget cannot spend on policing, protection or anything else in the same year.
