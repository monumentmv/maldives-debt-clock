Reading these figures

These figures come from the Ministry of Finance's Weekly Fiscal Developments report, published most weeks since December 2018. Each report gives totals from 1 January to its date, alongside the approved budget for the full year and the same date a year earlier. The site reads every report automatically and checks that its totals add up before using it.

Weekly figures are early. The Ministry notes that they change as accounts are reconciled, and spending is counted when it is recorded, not when cash is paid. Final figures for a year, such as those in MMA's monthly series used on the main page, can differ, sometimes by several billion rufiyaa. For the same reason, the year-by-year chart uses the last weekly report of each year, which is not the final audited outturn.

Spending here excludes loan repayments, investments and lending, which the Ministry lists separately. The gap between revenue and spending is the overall balance. Loan repayments come on top of it, so the amount the government has to borrow in a year is larger than the gap. The approved budget changes when Parliament passes a supplementary budget, and the reports use the latest approved figures. More in the methodology.
