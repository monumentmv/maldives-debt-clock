About this site

Maldives National Debt Clock shows how much the Maldivian government owes, what that means for each citizen, and how the government's income and spending compare. It is built entirely from official statistics published by Maldives Monetary Authority, and every figure is explained on the methodology page.

Public debt affects everyone. It shapes what the government can spend on schools, hospitals and islands, and what future generations will have to repay. The aim is to make those numbers easy to see and understand, without taking a political side.

The site is independent and is not affiliated with Maldives Monetary Authority, the Ministry of Finance or any political party.
