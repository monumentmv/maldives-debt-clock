All the data, in one file

Every figure on this site comes from four official publishers, the Maldives Monetary Authority (MMA), the Maldives Inland Revenue Authority (MIRA), the Ministry of Finance and the Maldives Police Service. They are combined here into one clean dataset, with plain names, consistent units and a note of where each series comes from. It is rebuilt every morning when new figures are published.

Download everything (Excel)One workbook with a catalogue of every series, a sheet for each frequency and the weekly budget tables. Download everything (CSV)The same data as plain CSV files, for R, Stata, Python or any other software.

What's inside

Search or filter the list to see every series, its unit and how far back it goes. Money is in millions of rufiyaa or US dollars, as shown.

Using this data

The data is free to use. Please credit the original publishers, MMA, MIRA and the Ministry of Finance, and the Maldives National Debt Clock. Figures are as published by each source, which can revise them later, and they are not adjusted for inflation. The methodology explains how each source is read and what it covers.

The individual files can also be opened one by one. These are the series catalogue, all series in one list, the weekly budget tables and cases reported to the police. Spotted a problem? Send a message through the form on the About page.
